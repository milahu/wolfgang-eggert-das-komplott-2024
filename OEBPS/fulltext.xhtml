The police department that serves the township where former

News of the leadership vacuum comes as experts and officials call for

and federal agents that allowed a shooter to hit Trump, kill one rally

Amid reports of apparent failures by Secret Service agents manning the

federal agencies likely placed disproportionate responsibility on local
police, said Jeffrey Fagan, a professor at Columbia University Law

“Local cops were left to shoulder the burden of security without much

anyone else,” he said. “They should have yelled for help, and so should
the county government leaders.”

The shooting has raised new questions in the debate over police
